## 6. AUTO-EXCLUSION CASES (Article 12)

1. Financial offer or entire technical offer submitted **outside TUNEPS**

3. Missing temporary financial guarantee document

---

## 7. EVALUATION CRITERIA

### Phase 1 — Financial Validation
- Verify administrative documents + temporary guarantee
- Verify financial offer documents
- Correct arithmetic/clerical errors
- **Rank all financial offers ascending**

### Phase 2 — Technical Conformity
- Verify technical compliance of the **lowest-priced bidder first**
- If non-conforming → excluded, then next lowest bidder evaluated
- Committee may request clarification within a deadline; failure to respond = move to next bid

### Phase 3 — Security Clearance
- Verify bidder's compliance with **national defense security requirements**

### Award Basis
- **Lowest technically conforming offer** (Prix le moins-disant)

---

## 8. PRICING & ADJUSTMENT RULES

### Pricing Basis
- Contract awarded at **unit prices**
- Payment calculated on **actually delivered quantities**
- Discrepancies between unit prices and individual price totals → **unit prices take precedence**
- Discrepancies between written and numeric amounts → **written amount takes precedence**

### Price Adjustments (Article 16)
- Prices are **fixed** for the contract duration
- Price adjustment allowed if the gap between submission date and contract notification exceeds **120 days**
- Adjustment formula:
  ```
  Updated Price = Original Price × (1 + Adjustment Rate)
  Adjustment Rate = (Elapsed Days × TMM Market Rate) / 365
  ```
- Per-call adjustment cap: **0.5/1000 per day**
- Total adjustment cap: **2% of original contract value**
