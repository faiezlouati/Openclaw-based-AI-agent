## 2. PARTICIPATION CONDITIONS

### Article 2 — Scope
- Single lot consisting of **8 line items**
- **Bidders must submit for the entire lot** — partial bids are not accepted

### Article 3 — Eligibility
Participating suppliers must demonstrate:
- **Professional qualifications** appropriate to the tender scope
- **Technical capabilities** sufficient to fulfill the requirements
- **Financial guarantees** as required by the procurement regulations

All three dimensions must be satisfied simultaneously.

### Article 4 — Access to Documents
All interested parties may download the complete tender package via the **TUNEPS online public procurement portal** upon publication of the call for competition.

## 5. SUBMISSION RULES — ARTICLE 8

### Online Submission (via TUNEPS)
- **Registration** in the TUNEPS online procurement system is **mandatory** before the submission deadline
- **Financial and technical offers** must be submitted via TUNEPS

### Late Submissions
> Bids received or modified after the submission deadline are automatically rejected.

No exceptions.

### Modifications
- Modifications are permitted during the submission window
- After the deadline: no modification, correction, addition, or withdrawal is allowed
- Bids containing additions or modifications must indicate the altered items by the person who signed the bid

### Partial Submission / Out-of-Line Documents
If the technical bid exceeds the maximum volume allowed online, bidders may:
- Submit a **partial technical bid online** within the submission window
- Send the **remaining technical documents out-of-line** (offline)
- The offline submission must include, in the electronic bid, a list of all documents sent offline

## 7. EVALUATION — ARTICLE 13

| Phase | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **Stage 1** | Administrative compliance check + financial guarantee verification |
| **Stage 2** | Financial offer correction — arithmetic and material errors corrected where necessary; offers ranked ascending |
| **Stage 3** | Lowest evaluated compliant bid wins (Moins-disant) |

> Evaluation is based on the **Lowest Evaluated Compliant Bid** (Moins-disant) principle — technically compliant offers ranked by price, with the lowest price winning, provided all administrative and technical requirements are met.

---

## 8. EXCLUSION CONDITIONS — ARTICLE 12

Bids are **automatically excluded** if:
1. Financial offer or complete technical offer submitted outside TUNEPS
2. Bid received after the submission deadline
3. Bid does not include the temporary financial guarantee document
